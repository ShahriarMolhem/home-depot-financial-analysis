## Design
- 16:9 PowerPoint, white/navy with a restrained orange accent.
- All numerical charts are drawn from figures reconciled to the model.
- Speaker notes accompany each slide.
- No professional certification, audit report, or share-price recommendation is claimed.

## Speaking guidance
1. Explain **reported historical facts first**, before forecast assumptions.
2. Distinguish **simple FCF** (operating cash flow minus capital expenditures) from forecast **unlevered FCFF**.
3. Explain why fiscal 2024's 53 weeks and acquisitions make some comparisons imperfect.
4. Explain why a DCF with 77.6% terminal-value share is sensitive to discount assumptions.
5. If asked about today's share price, explain why a FY2025-date model is not a current price target.
