## Projection classification

The financial forecasts, forward margins, cash tax rate, WACC and terminal growth are **analyst assumptions**; they are not company-reported projections. The FY2025 historical baseline is reported historical fact.

## Why the sensitivity analysis is necessary

A large part of the calculated present value comes from terminal FCFF. A 1.5 percentage point WACC increase with constant 2.5% g reduces per-share model value materially; the opposite change increases value. The sensitivity table is a transparent *what-if* exercise, not an estimated probability distribution.
