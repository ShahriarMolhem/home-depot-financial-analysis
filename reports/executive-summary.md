# Executive Summary | The Home Depot Financial Performance & Valuation

**Shahriar Molhem** · Independent undergraduate BBA Finance portfolio · University of Georgia  
**Historical period:** FY2021–FY2025. **Valuation reference:** FY2025 year-end (February 1, 2026).  
**Audience:** Finance, FP&A, and accounting internship recruiters.

## The question

Home Depot increased reported sales between FY2021 and FY2025. Did operating profitability and cash generation improve at the same pace? How does a simple, assumption-driven free-cash-flow valuation respond to changes in discount rate and perpetual growth?

## Key findings

| Measure | FY2021 | FY2025 | Interpretation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Net sales | $151.157bn | $164.683bn | 8.9% cumulative reported growth |
| Operating income | $23.040bn | $20.890bn | 9.3% below FY2021 |
| Operating margin | 15.2% | 12.7% | Approximately 2.6 percentage points lower |
| Operating cash flow | $16.571bn | $16.325bn | Volatility across the intervening years |
| Simple FCF (OCF − capex) | $14.005bn | $12.646bn | Not the same as unlevered FCFF |

The analysis highlights a difference between reported sales growth and operating income performance. It does **not** attribute all changes to a single cause; acquisitions, fiscal-year length, and operating cost movements complicate interpretation.

## Illustrative valuation, not a stock-price target

An educational five-year FCFF discounted cash flow model uses assumptions of **8.0% WACC**, **2.5% perpetual growth**, and a gradual operating-margin recovery. It calculates **$318.9bn enterprise value** and an **illustrative $265.54 per share** after the financial-debt bridge. About **77.6% of enterprise value** comes from the discounted terminal value, making the output sensitive to assumptions. Neither WACC nor long-run growth is presented as a measured market fact.

## Why it matters for finance internships

This work demonstrates reading SEC financial statements, calculating historical performance measures, organizing a formula-linked Excel model, documenting forecast assumptions, performing sensitivity analysis, and communicating meaningful limitations.

## Important limitations

- FY2024 had 53 weeks; acquisition changes (SRS and GMS) complicate reported growth comparisons.
- The DCF's WACC and other forecasting assumptions are illustrative, not independently market-calibrated.
- The enterprise-to-equity bridge does not fully reflect leases or all possible nonoperating adjustments.
- Financial statements and this model do not constitute an independent audit or certified valuation.
- This study is dated to FY2025 year-end; **it is not a current buy/sell rating**.
