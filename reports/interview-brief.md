# Interview Preparation Brief | 90-second Walkthrough

## Elevator summary (sample to adapt in your own words)

> I analyzed Home Depot's SEC financial statements over five fiscal years and found that reported sales increased while operating margin declined. I built an Excel model to calculate historical ratios and cash-flow measures, then used a simplified five-year free-cash-flow valuation with clearly labeled assumptions. The main takeaway was that the valuation result changes substantially with discount rate and long-run growth, so I presented sensitivities and important limits instead of treating the output as a precise price target.

## Questions to prepare for

**Why is reported sales growth not the full story?**  
Operating income and operating margin moved differently from sales; compare profitability alongside revenue. FY2024 had 53 weeks, and acquisition changes influence reported growth.

**What is the difference between simple FCF and FCFF?**  
Simple FCF here is reported operating cash flow less capital spending. FCFF begins with after-tax operating profit, adds D&A, subtracts capital investment and the change in operating working capital; it is used to value the enterprise before financing.

**What drives your valuation most?**  
The forecast assumptions, 8% WACC, 2.5% long-run growth, and especially terminal value (77.6% of enterprise value).

**Did you estimate WACC from beta and current market yields?**  
No. 8% is a transparent academic assumption. A more advanced study would calculate a market-implied or independently estimated WACC.

**Is $265.54 the stock price today?**  
No. It is the model's output at FY2025 year-end under its assumptions; it is not a current market-price estimate or investment recommendation.

**What would you improve?**  
A market-derived discount rate, detailed acquisition-normalized analysis, debt/lease adjustments, operating segment detail, and comprehensive financial-statement forecasting.

**Did you do an audit or use nonpublic company information?**  
No. The work is an independent student portfolio analysis based on public SEC reporting. It is not CPA-reviewed or issuer-endorsed.

## Integrity
The owner should understand the model and confidently explain these answers **before** claiming the work on a resume or discussing it in a job interview. AI assistance used in producing drafts or spreadsheets should not be mistaken for independent review or demonstrated mastery.
